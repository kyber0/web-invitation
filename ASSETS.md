# Galaxy invitation assets

`public/sapphire-ribbon.png` is the selected transparent satin ribbon asset, generated using the built-in imagegen tool. It is used on the envelope, portrait frame, and attire section. Its original generated output is preserved; the original portrait remains unchanged by this design update.

Final generation prompt:

> Asset type: transparent cutout decorative asset for an elegant eighteenth birthday invitation website. One luxurious midnight navy and sapphire blue silk satin bow, with two beautifully folded loops, a tightly cinched center knot and two long flowing corkscrew curled ribbon tails descending downwards. Front view, bow in top third, tails reaching bottom edge, entire object visible with generous transparent margin. Photorealistic silk material with luminous cool blue highlights on folds and deep black-blue shadows; tiny sparse silver glitter flecks only along edges. Refined, rich, glamorous evening invitation aesthetic. Isolated on genuinely transparent background, no paper, no border, no text, no letters, no person, no ornaments beyond the bow and its curling ribbons. Tall portrait composition. High detail.

`public/crystal.svg` is a code-native silver-and-sapphire ornament. `public/effects.js` renders the galaxy stars and stardust particles directly in a canvas. Decorative imagery is hidden from assistive technology. The effects honor reduced-motion preferences and can be paused by guests.
